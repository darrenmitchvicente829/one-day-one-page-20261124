# Sources and dates

The page covers Tuesday 24 November 2026. A story is on it only if it happened, or was first reported, on that
day, by UTC or by the local date where it happened. This first edition went to press at 07:30 UTC on the day.

Tags: **confirmed** (two or more outlets, or a final result), **official** (on record from an authority),
**reported** (carried by the outlets named), **claim** (one side's account, unverified), **due** (scheduled
for later the same day and not yet reported as done).
