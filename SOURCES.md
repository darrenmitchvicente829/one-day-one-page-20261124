The page covers Tuesday 24 November 2026. A story is on it only if it happened, or was first reported, on that
day, by UTC or by the local date where it happened. This is the full-day edition: it closed at 22:30 UTC on the
day, and every item is given as it stood then. An earlier edition went to press at 07:30 UTC.

Tags: **confirmed** (two or more outlets, or a final result), **official** (on record from an authority),
**reported** (carried by the outlets named), **claim** (one side's account, unverified).
